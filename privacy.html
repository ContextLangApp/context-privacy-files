Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Applies to the Context app for iOS and Android.

This policy explains what Context collects, why, and how to delete it. If anything here is unclear, contact admin@context-lang.com.

Who we are

Context is operated by Ashkan Abedi (“we”, “us”), Hochschule Darmstadt, Schöfferstraße 3, 64295 Darmstadt, Germany. We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, and a securely hashed password if you sign up with email. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google instead — we never see your Google password.
  • Your learning profile. The display name, learning goal, favourite topics and target German level (A1–C2) you choose during onboarding, and any later edits.
  • Your learning content. Words you save to your Dictionary along with their generated definitions and examples, the daily lessons generated for you, and a record of which lessons you completed and when (this drives your streak, daily goal and progress charts).
  • Limited product analytics. When you tap a “Pro” upsell we record the tap and its timestamp, so we can gauge interest in a paid tier. We do not use advertising identifiers, and there are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.
  • Daily usage counts. How many AI requests your account made each day (lessons, feedback, speech), so we can apply fair-use limits. These counts are deleted with your account.
  • On your device only. A flag recording which first-use tutorials you have already dismissed, and the daily “your lesson pack is ready” reminder, which is scheduled by your phone itself. Neither leaves your device, and both are removed when you uninstall the app.

Microphone and voice recordings

Speaking and conversation lessons need your microphone. The microphone is only accessed while you are actively recording in a lesson.

  • Speaking lessons use your phone’s built-in speech recognition — Apple Speech Recognition on iPhone, or your device’s speech service on Android — to show your words as you speak. Depending on your device and settings, the provider may process the audio on its own servers, under its own privacy terms.
  • Conversation lessons send your recorded answer over an encrypted connection to Microsoft Azure Speech, which converts it to text and returns the transcript.
  • We do not store your audio recordings. Audio is passed straight to the speech service and discarded; only the resulting text comes back to the app.
  • The transcript is used to generate your feedback during that lesson. It is not saved to your account unless you choose to save a word or correction to your Dictionary.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • To generate lessons, speaking prompts and conversation scenarios matched to your level and chosen topics.
  • To give you feedback on your grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.
  • To store your Dictionary and show your progress, streak and daily goal.
  • To understand which features people want, in aggregate.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to serve advertising.

Who processes your data on our behalf

  • Supabase — hosting, authentication and the database that stores your account, profile, Dictionary and lesson history.
  • Microsoft Azure — Azure Speech converts your voice to text and reads text aloud; Azure AI Foundry generates lesson content and feedback. Lesson text, prompts and transcripts are sent to these services to produce your lessons. Under Microsoft’s terms, content may be retained briefly for abuse monitoring and is not used to train their models.
  • Apple (iPhone) or your device’s speech provider (Android) — speech recognition in speaking lessons, as described above.
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google, to authenticate you. The app also downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which lets Google see your device’s IP address; no account information is sent with that request.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own terms.

How long we keep it

Your account and learning data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, it and everything attached to it — profile, saved words, generated lessons, completion history, usage counts and analytics events — are permanently deleted from our database immediately. This cannot be undone, and we do not keep a backup copy for you to restore.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in two ways:

  • In the app — Profile → Account → Delete account.
  • On the web, without the app installed — use the account deletion page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time, and delete everything using the links above. For anything else, email admin@context-lang.com and we will respond within one month.

Children

Context is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with per-user access rules, so one account cannot read another’s data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
